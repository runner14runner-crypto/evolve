Questions.

Is connecting my wallet safe?

The site only asks your wallet for its public address, then reads your $EVOLVE balance from the Solana chain. It never requests a signature, a transaction or an approval. If anything claiming to be $EVOLVE ever asks you to sign, close it.

How do I earn XP?

Holding earns XP every minute, multiplied by your egg tier, even with the tab closed. When we see your balance go up, you get a buy bonus. When it goes down, you lose XP and it counts against you at Level 5.

How is my egg tier decided?

By your live balance: any amount gets a Pink egg, 1M gets Silver, 5M gets Gold and 10M (1% of supply) gets Diamond. Higher tiers grow faster and get better Level 5 odds. Drop below a threshold and your tier drops with it.

How is my final form picked?

It's a weighted roll at Level 5. Never selling boosts the Diamond Dragon. Selling and then buying back boosts the Rug Phoenix. Selling a lot pulls you toward the Paper Ghost. The Dev starts at 1 in 1,000, and a Diamond egg raises that to 1 in 200.

Is $EVOLVE an investment?

No. It's a parody meme project made for fun, with no utility and no promise of anything. Never put in money you can't afford to lose.
