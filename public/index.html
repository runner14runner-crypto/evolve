Questions.

Does this touch my real wallet?

No. The hatchery is a simulator. The address you enter only decides your creature's name. Nothing is signed, read from chain or sent anywhere, and progress lives in your browser.

How do I earn XP?

Buying and diamond-handing earn XP. Holding a bag earns passive XP over time, even with the tab closed. Aping fresh launches is high risk, high reward. Panic selling costs XP and counts against you at Level 5.

How is my final form picked?

It's a weighted roll at Level 5. Never selling boosts the Diamond Dragon. Surviving rugs boosts the Rug Phoenix. Selling a lot pulls you toward the Paper Ghost. The Dev is 1 in 1,000 no matter what you do.

Is $EVOLVE an investment?

No. It's a parody meme project made for fun, with no utility and no promise of anything. Never put in money you can't afford to lose.
