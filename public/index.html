FAQ

Questions.

Does this connect to my real wallet?

No. The lab is a simulator. It uses the address you type only as a seed for your creature's look and name. Nothing is signed, read from chain, or sent anywhere. Progress is saved in your browser.

How does XP work?

Buying and holding earn XP. Holding a bag earns passive XP over time, even when the tab is closed. Panic selling costs XP and counts against you when your final form is rolled. Aping new launches is high variance.

How is the Level 5 form chosen?

It is a weighted roll. Never selling raises your Diamond Dragon odds. Surviving rugs raises your Phoenix odds. Lots of paper-hand sells push you toward the Ghost. The Dev is 1 in 1,000 no matter what.

Is $EVOLVE an investment?

No. $EVOLVE is a parody meme project made for fun. It has no utility, no roadmap and no expectation of return. Do not buy anything you cannot afford to lose.
